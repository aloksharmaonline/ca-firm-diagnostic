# Validation Session — your script (Week 3, ~45 min, you + founder)

**Goal:** he pressure-tests the root-cause map against reality, approves/kill interventions, locks the baseline. He leaves owning the plan.

**Bring:** `05_root-cause-map.md` (filled) + `06_intervention-table.md` (filled) + `07_dashboard.md` baseline row + workbook on screen.

---

## 1. Open with his words, not ours (5 min)
Read back: his 12-month goal, his vision line, his lag target (Dashboard B2/B3). Ask: *"Still true, or has anything shifted?"* — fixes headline if needed.

## 2. Show the Team Opinion findings first — he hasn't seen them (10 min)
Dimension scores + flags + themes (anonymity protocol: percentages and themes only; psych-safety mean and the two flagged dimensions are the headline). Then ask, in order:
- *"Which of these surprises you most?"*
- *"Which do you disagree with?"* — disagreement is data: if he rejects a Team Opinion-strong finding, it enters #8 as confirmed-with-dissent, noted, NOT removed.

## 3. Walk the root-cause table row by row (15 min)
For each cause: read it, show its streams, ask **"true, partly, or no?"**
- **True** → row stays Confirmed.
- **Partly** → downgrade one confidence level; if it drops to WEAK, it's parked (no intervention).
- **No** → challenge him: *"what does the evidence look like from where you sit?"* If his counter-evidence is solid, strike the row. If it's just opinion against anonymous data, the row stays, dissent noted.
- Trigger the fallback chat with 2–3 staff only if a decision-hinging row dies this way.

## 4. Structural flags — deliver plainly (5 min)
The footer items (comp / progression policy / revenue model): *"These matter, but they're yours to decide — I flag, I don't fix."* Note which he accepts.

## 5. Interventions: approve, kill, assign (10 min)
Each #9 row: owner accepts? start date? proof metric sensible?
- Kill anything without a willing owner — a row nobody owns is a wish.
- Fewer than 4 surviving rows is a **good outcome**, not a failure.

## 6. Close: lock baseline + next date (5 min)
- Fill `07_dashboard.md` baseline row with today's numbers, date it, both sign off verbally.
- Fix month-3 Team Opinion re-run date and month-6 review date **before leaving the room.**

---

**Post-session (you, 30 min):** apply the agreed verdicts to #8/#9, fill the #10 baseline row → send founder a one-page recap (priority, causes, owners, dates) → share the session outcomes with me for the final analysis record.
